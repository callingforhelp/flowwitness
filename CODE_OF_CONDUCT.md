# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive in issues, reviews and other project spaces. Welcome people with different backgrounds and skill levels. Critique ideas and behavior without personal attacks.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing and posting private customer data are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove content, limit participation or ban accounts, proportionate to the behavior and repeated violations.

Use GitHub's report-content controls for confidential reports of abusive content; avoid repeating sensitive material in public issues. The maintainer @callingforhelp handles project moderation and may use GitHub's safety process when necessary. There is no dedicated private community-reporting inbox at this stage.

## 中文说明

请尊重不同背景与经验的参与者，具体、友善地讨论问题。禁止骚扰、歧视、威胁、人身攻击和公开隐私。维护者可按情节删除内容、限制参与或封禁；敏感举报可使用 GitHub 内容举报机制，勿在公开 issue 重复隐私材料。当前无独立私密社区举报邮箱。
